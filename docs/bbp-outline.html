TITLE
Reproduce the Capability, Not the Founder
A performance-consulting concept, with supporting software, for transferring expert know-how in founder-led businesses.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role Point A
A founder-led service business is growing, and key work still escalates to the founder. You're an owner, advisor, or builder who wants the team to perform without constant founder help. Expertise lives in one head, and documenting it rarely makes people capable.
TRANSITION 1
Knowledge Harvest starts with the problem, then the tools. Three ideas explain it: the approach, the software, and the supporting material.
