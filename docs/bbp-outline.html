TITLE
Reproduce the Capability, Not the Founder
A performance-consulting concept, with supporting software, for transferring expert know-how in founder-led businesses.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role
A founder-led service business is growing, and key work still escalates to the founder. You're an owner, advisor, or builder who wants the team to perform without constant founder help.
Point A (Where am I?) Call to Action (How do I get from A to B?) Point B (Where do I want to be?)
Expertise lives in one head, and documenting it rarely makes people capable. Review the hypothesis, run the prototype, and try a playbook on a real procedure. A founder who can step back because the capability is reproducible.
