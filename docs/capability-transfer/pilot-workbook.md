## 4. Expert capture interview

Choose one ordinary case, one exception, and one misleading near-match. Record artifact references and timestamps where available.

1. Walk through what happened in this actual case, in order.
2. Point to the first detail that changed your interpretation.
3. Name the plausible alternatives you rejected and the evidence against them.
4. Show what a novice might notice instead. What would that mistake cost?
5. Identify the rule, standard, or source you relied on and its limits.
6. Change one detail: when would your decision change?
7. Specify when an employee must stop or escalate, including missing information.
8. Describe what satisfactory independent work would look like.

For each extracted rule: [rule ID, wording, source reference, scope, exceptions, unresolved disagreement, expert approval and date]. Conflicting experts trigger clarification; do not merge conflicting advice into a confident rule.
