# Background layer prompts

Generated by `kit/prompt_bg.py --publish` from `prompts/backgrounds.spec.json` and `config/theme.json`. Landscape, morning light. Portrait numbers and other light settings: `kit/prompt_bg.py --help`.

## How to use

1. Open a new chat and make the MASTER scene first. Ask for 4 variations and pick one; the pick decides the look of every layer.
2. In the SAME chat, attach the master you picked and paste layer 1, then layer 2, and so on, one at a time. Keeping one chat is what keeps the layers matching.
3. If your tool cannot attach an image, say 'the scene above' instead of 'the attached scene'.
4. Check each layer: the base lines match the master, the stage strip is empty, the middle is calm, and the edges against the magenta are crisp (no glow). Ask for a retake of just that layer if not.
5. Send the layers back in the chat (or import them with kit/import_backdrop.py). They stack in this order, back to front: sky, clouds, far, near, ground, back, front.
6. To mix and match, use layers from different environments: every set shares the same horizon, floor line and depth rules, so far layers from one scene sit on the ground of another. Keep the LIGHT line the same across the sets you combine.
