Before you start

  1. Open an exercise below and tap Copy on the prompt.
  2. If your tool accepts a reference image, attach the standing reference strip and use the first prompt. If not, use the second (character in words).
  3. Make it wide, 16:9, at least about 1300 pixels across. Ask for 4 takes.
  4. Pick the take whose form matches the plan's wording, not the prettiest one.
